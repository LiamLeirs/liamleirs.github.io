I hold a **Master’s degree in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence from the University of Antwerp, obtained with distinction**, alongside a strong academic background in computer science.

My main interests include:

- Machine Learning & Artificial Intelligence
- Recommender Systems
- Computer Vision
- Reinforcement Learning
- Explainable AI
- Data Analytics

I enjoy working at the intersection of **research and software engineering**, turning ideas into working systems and evaluating them through carefully designed experiments.

My projects span areas such as recommender systems, computer vision, model optimization, and reinforcement learning, with a particular interest in understanding not only **whether** a model works, but also **why** it works and how it can be improved.
